Before You Start

Common questions from warehouse teams.

Can we start without moving every customer at once?

Yes. Start with one warehouse workflow or customer program, validate it, then expand deliberately.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

The Launch Warehouse plan continues at $129 per month unless canceled before the trial ends.

What if our operation needs customer portals or integrations?

Book a guided demo so the rollout can account for customer access, Shopify, SFTP, APIs, EDI, billing, and multi-warehouse needs.

Will our customers see one another's information?

No. Customer access and warehouse inventory are scoped by company and warehouse, with server-side permission checks.
